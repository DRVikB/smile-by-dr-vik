Questions

Is the concept a treatment outcome?

No. It’s a visual concept to support the conversation, not a diagnosis, treatment plan or guaranteed result. Clinical assessment always comes first.

Where are patient photos kept?

Cases save on your device first. When you’re signed in they sync to your account in private storage, so you can reopen them on your other devices; nobody else can see them. The Case Library holds the finished cases you choose to add, and it’s private to you too.

Do I need an account to try it?

No. Sample patients let you explore every step without an account. Creating AI concepts needs a free account and a SmileCompose Pro plan, which starts with a 3-day free trial.

Which devices does it work on?

iPad and iPhone, plus any modern browser on a Mac or PC. One account and one plan cover them all.

Will patients expect exactly this result?

Every concept is labelled as an AI visualisation, not a guaranteed result, on screen and in everything you share. It’s a way to agree a direction together; your clinical plan and the final treatment decide the outcome.

How do I get the patient’s consent?

Before a photo is used, you confirm you’re authorised to process it for the consultation. Record consent the way you normally do in your practice.

Does it replace my digital smile design workflow?

No. SmileCompose is for the consultation: helping a patient see what’s possible and decide. Your clinical records, scans, mock-ups and lab work carry on as normal.

Can my team use it?

Each SmileCompose account belongs to one clinician, with its own cases, Case Library and allowance. Team members can present a case on your iPad with you, or use their own account.

What counts as a generation?

Each AI concept you create uses one. Compare 3 shapes uses three. Whitening, comparing, Smile analysis, sharing and saving never use one.
