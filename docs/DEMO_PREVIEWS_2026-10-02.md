# Prepared demo photographs

Built with the built-in image-generation tool from the existing fictional demo portrait. These are prepared AI illustrations, not evidence of achievable clinical results.

Thirteen lossless WebP portraits cover all nine combinations of Square / Round / Tapered and single-shade composite / layered composite / porcelain; one original-shade alignment-only example; and three full-arch zirconia forms. No provider requests or credits are used when viewing these examples in test mode. The usual original-lip/face protection still runs when presenting them.

The demo does not simulate shade, tooth-count changes, individual-tooth plans, clinician notes, fine sliders, lower-only treatment or provisional full-arch treatment. Hidden lower teeth must stay hidden. Alignment plus restoration uses the matching prepared restorative photograph; it is not a separate orthodontic simulation.

- Selector regressions verify separate files for all nine material/form pairs, treatment precedence, legacy Composite mapping, distinct zirconia forms and unsupported choices.
- All 404 tests passed; lint, TypeScript, the production build and the synced iOS bundle verification passed.
- Desktop WebKit ran all 13 examples through the actual alignment and face-protection pipeline: all results were distinct, protection succeeded for each, and the tested upper-face region had no changed pixels.
- The in-app three-shape comparison returned Square, Rounded and Triangular as three distinct images, with zero paid requests recorded.

- Inspect enlarged smiles before presenting these material examples; their differences are illustrative and do not define how every restoration of that material must look.
