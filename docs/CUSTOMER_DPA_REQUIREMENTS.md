# Customer data processing agreement: requirements

> **REQUIRES SOLICITOR DRAFTING.** This lists what the agreement must cover so it matches how the product works. It is not the agreement. Do not invent liability, indemnity, jurisdiction or commercial terms; a solicitor must draft them.

Where SmileCompose processes patient data on a practice's behalf (see [DATA_ROLE_MAP.md](DATA_ROLE_MAP.md)), UK GDPR Art. 28(3) requires a written contract. The contract must cover the following.

## Mandatory Art. 28(3) content

| Term | How the product meets it today | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| Subject matter, duration, nature, purpose, data types, data subjects | Generation of illustrative visualisations; adult patients; photos and clinical inputs | Draft the schedule |
| Process only on documented instructions | Processing happens only when the clinician presses Generate and confirms; no secondary use | — |
| Confidentiality of personnel | [OWNER: staff confidentiality undertakings] | Owner |
| Security (Art. 32) | [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) | — |
| Sub-processors: prior general authorisation, notice of changes, flow-down terms | [SUBPROCESSORS.md](SUBPROCESSORS.md) | Notification mechanism (e.g. email plus a notice page) |
| Assistance with data subject rights | Patient data lives on the practice's device (export/delete in-app); we hold no patient images | Document the assistance procedure |
| Assistance with security, breach notification, DPIA, prior consultation | [INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md](INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md), [DPIA.md](DPIA.md) template | Set the breach-notification timescale to the customer |
| Deletion or return at end of service | No server-side patient data; device data deleted by the customer; account deletion in-app | — |
| Information and audit rights | [OWNER DECISION: audit approach, e.g. documentation plus a questionnaire] | Owner / solicitor |
| Infringing-instruction notification | — | Include |

## Product-specific terms to include

- **International transfers:** Google processing may be outside the UK. List the mechanisms ([INTERNATIONAL_TRANSFERS.md](INTERNATIONAL_TRANSFERS.md)).
- **AI provider logging:** limited abuse-monitoring retention by Google; no training use.
- **Customer obligations:** lawful basis and Art. 9 condition; patient privacy information; adults only in V1; no unnecessary identifiers in notes or case references; device security (passcode, OS updates, MDM where available); library-style decisions.
- **Acceptance record:** the app has a `customer_dpa` consent record type (`consent_records`) ready to record DPA acceptance with a version. **Gap:** there is no DPA acceptance UI yet, because the DPA text does not exist.
- **Clinical responsibility and the medical device boundary** (see [MEDICAL_DEVICE_BOUNDARY.md](MEDICAL_DEVICE_BOUNDARY.md)).

## Not to be generated by engineering

Warranties, indemnities, liability caps, governing law, jurisdiction, medical liability, and commercial terms such as fees, term and termination. These are **LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED** placeholders in `src/legal/termsOfService.ts`.
