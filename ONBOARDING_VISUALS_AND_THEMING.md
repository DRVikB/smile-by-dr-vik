# Onboarding visuals and Light / Dark theming

Status: implemented (2026-09-28). Verified in the browser at iPhone and iPad sizes in Light and Dark, and on the iOS 27 simulator (iPhone 18 Pro Max, iPad Pro 11") for the launch sequence.

## Onboarding visual system

`src/components/onboarding/OnboardingVisuals.tsx` + `src/app/onboarding.css`. Built from SmileCompose's own sample case (cropped test-mode images in `public/onboarding/`) and app UI — no stock imagery — and themed only through tokens, so every visual works in Light and Dark. Decorative motion is removed under Reduce Motion.

| Step | Visual |
|---|---|
| Welcome | Before/after reveal card with an animated split, a layered plate and two design callouts ("Porcelain · BL2", "Concept visualisation") |
| Account | Account card: medallion, iPhone / iPad / Web, "Patient cases stay on this device" |
| Personalise | Live greeting preview on the Home hero, with the profile photo when added; optional "Add profile photo" (signed in) |
| How It Works | Capture → Compose → Visualise → Share mini-screens |
| Your style (Case Library) | Finished cases → SMILECOMPOSE → new patient design (smile-region crops `public/onboarding/case-*.jpg`, `new-design.jpg`); after adding, the clinician's own cases replace the samples |
| Subscription | Pro value visual with the reveal and plan callouts |
| Ready | Drawn check, then the personalised workspace rising into view |

Layout (`Frame`): iPhone — headline, visual, actions in one column; iPad portrait — visual panel across the top; iPad landscape — visual panel left, content right. Short landscape heights shrink the type.

## Theme architecture

- **Tokens** — `src/app/theme.css`: semantic roles (`--background`, `--bg-grouped`, `--card`, `--surface*`, `--glass*`, `--text-primary/secondary/tertiary`, `--accent*`, `--border*`, `--separator`, `--destructive*`, `--selected-fill`, `--scrim`, `--shadow*`, `--mark-filter`, onboarding `--ob-*`…) defined for Light (`:root`) and Dark (`:root[data-theme="dark"]`). Components use roles, not raw colours. Photographs are never tinted.
- **Preference** — `src/lib/appearance.ts`: System (default) / Light / Dark, stored in `localStorage` `smile.appearance`. `APPEARANCE_BOOT_SCRIPT` sets `data-theme` before first paint (no flash). System follows `prefers-color-scheme` live. Settings › Appearance (`AppearanceSettings.tsx`) offers the three options with previews.
- **Accessibility** — Increase Contrast (`prefers-contrast: more` and `data-increase-contrast` from iOS) strengthens borders, secondary text and separators; Reduce Transparency (`prefers-reduced-transparency` and `data-reduce-transparency` from iOS) replaces glass with solid surfaces and removes blur; Reduce Motion removes decorative animation.

## Native Apple appearance behaviour

- `AppearancePlugin.swift` applies the choice with `overrideUserInterfaceStyle`, so the status bar, share sheet, photo picker and Sign in with Apple match the app, and reports iOS Increase Contrast / Reduce Transparency to the page.
- `SmileComposeBridgeViewController` paints the native background ivory / deep charcoal (`SmileComposeColors`) so launch, rotation and overscroll never flash white.
- **Launch screen** (`ios/App/App/Base.lproj/LaunchScreen.storyboard`): the SmileCompose symbol, the SMILECOMPOSE wordmark and "Designed By" with the Dr Vik logo, on `LaunchBackground` (ivory / charcoal). Text uses the named colours `LaunchText` and `LaunchSubtle`, which follow Light and Dark. The symbol and Dr Vik logo are bundled PNGs in brand bronze (`ios/App/App/Launch/`, generated by `swift scripts/render-launch-artwork.swift`), which read on both backgrounds.
  - Why not asset-catalog images or tracked attributed text: on the iOS 27 simulator the launch renderer drew neither (verified from the system's launch snapshots), which is also why the previous launch screen showed only "Designed by Dr Vik". Plain labels and bundled PNGs render reliably; the wordmark's tracking uses thin spaces.
  - The bridge controller keeps the same launch view over the web view until the page has loaded (max 4 s), then fades it; the page's first paint is the matching web `BrandLaunch` (same bronze artwork and sizes), so startup goes launch → app with no blank frame.
- The launch colours and the web theme colours match (`THEME_COLORS`, `theme-color` meta, `LaunchBackground`).
